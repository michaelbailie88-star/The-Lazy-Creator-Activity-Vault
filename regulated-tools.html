🧘 RegulatED Tool Kit

Tatianna Bailie · RegulatED

Four interactive SEL tools. Everything works offline and prints cleanly — printed copies show all content expanded.

The 4 Practices — tap to check off as you practice each one

✓Whole-body listening

Eyes watching, ears open, body still and turned toward the speaker.

✓Wait for the finish

Let the speaker finish their whole idea before you respond.

✓Say it back

Repeat what you heard in your own words: "So you're saying…"

✓Ask one good question

Show curiosity: "What happened next?" "How did that feel?"

The 6 Levels of Listening

1
Ignoring — not listening at all
2
Pretend listening — nodding, mind elsewhere
3
Selective listening — hearing only parts
4
Attentive listening — focused on the words
5
Active listening — words + feelings, responding
6
Empathic listening — understanding the other person's world

The 8 Strategies

🗣️ Use your words

Say what you want or how you feel, calmly and clearly.

🤝 Share or take turns

Make a plan: "You go first, then me."

🚶 Walk away

Leave the situation and find something else to do.

🙋 Ask for help

Find a teacher or trusted adult when it's too big to solve alone.

🧊 Cool down first

Take 3 deep breaths or get a drink of water before solving.

👂 Listen to the other side

Let the other person explain how they see it.

🎲 Try a compromise

Find something in the middle that works for both of you.

😔 Say sorry & repair

Apologize and do something kind to fix it.

Tap any strategy to expand it

🎈 Balloon breathing

Breathe in slowly through your nose and puff your belly out like a balloon filling. Breathe out slowly through your mouth as it deflates. Repeat 5 times.

🖐️ Five-finger breathing

Trace your hand: breathe in going up each finger, out coming down. One full hand = five calm breaths.

🐢 Turtle tuck

Pull your arms in, tuck your chin, and imagine going into your shell. Count to ten in your shell, then slowly come out.

🍋 Lemon squeeze

Pretend to squeeze lemons in your fists as hard as you can for 5 seconds, then drop them and let your hands go floppy. Repeat 3 times.

🧱 Wall push

Push against a wall as hard as you can for 10 seconds, like you're pushing it over. Heavy work calms busy bodies.

🦋 Butterfly hug

Cross your arms over your chest and gently tap your shoulders one at a time, slow like butterfly wings.

🔢 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Brings your brain back to now.

🧊 Cool-down sip

Take slow small sips of water and feel it cool you down from the inside.

🐍 Snake breath

Breathe in through your nose, then hiss the air out slowly like a snake — sssss — as long as you can.

⭐ Starfish stretch

Stand up and stretch your arms and legs out wide like a starfish. Hold for 5, then relax. Repeat 3 times.

🪨 Heavy rocks

Pretend to lift heavy rocks: squat, scoop, lift, and set them down slowly. Five big rocks.

🎧 Quiet corner minute

Go to the calm corner, hold a soft toy or fidget, and stay for one quiet minute before rejoining.

Tap a zone to see feelings and what helps

🔵 Blue Zone — low energy

Feelings: sad, tired, sick, bored, moving slowly.
What helps: rest, a hug, a stretch, water, talking to a trusted adult, gentle movement to re-energize.

🟢 Green Zone — ready to learn

Feelings: calm, happy, focused, proud, ready.
What helps: keep going! Notice what it feels like so you can find your way back here.

🟡 Yellow Zone — revving up

Feelings: frustrated, worried, silly, wiggly, excited, losing a little control.
What helps: balloon breathing, wall push, a movement break, naming the feeling out loud.

🔴 Red Zone — out of control

Feelings: angry, terrified, yelling, hitting, exploding.
What helps: stop and get space, adult support, turtle tuck, lemon squeeze — talk about it after, when back in green.

All zones are okay. The goal isn't to always be green — it's to notice which zone you're in and know one tool that helps.
