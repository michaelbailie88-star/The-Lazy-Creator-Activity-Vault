Honest answers

Questions you'd actually ask.

Is this a subscription? +

No. One-time payment. Lifetime access. No recurring charges, no auto-renewal, no "free trial" that secretly bills you. Pay once, log in forever.

What's the difference between the Vault and the School license? +

The Vault ($67) is for one person — a parent, educator, counsellor, or family. You get everything: all 5,330 activities across all 5 modules, the full 363 ParentED strategies, Mrs. Bailie the AI coach, and lifetime updates. The School license ($397) is for organizations — daycares, camps, after-school programs, schools — and includes 10 staff license keys so your team can each unlock it on their own device (need more seats? email us before buying), a printable educator quick-start PDF for your training binder, and direct email support from me with answers within 24 hours. Running a program? Email thelazycreatorco@gmail.com if you have questions before buying.

Can I see the dashboard before I buy? +

Yes. The live dashboard has one-click demo buttons (Vault and School) — no signup, no card. Click around, switch brands, open activity cards, see exactly what you're paying for. The activity card on this page is the real component, randomly pulled from the catalog.

Who is this actually for? +

AdaptED: early childhood educators, homeschool families, child care providers, YMCA program leaders, childcare workers. CampED: summer camp counselors, after-school program leaders, scout troop leaders, youth group volunteers. TeenagED: middle school teachers, parents of pre-teens, life-skills educators, mentors. RegulatED: educators and parents focused on emotional regulation and social-emotional development. ParentED: any parent or caregiver who wants practical home strategies backed by 20 years of early childhood experience.

Are these activities actually original? +

The library started with a core of seed activities written from real classroom and camp experience, then was systematically expanded — with AI assistance under the direction of a Senior Childhood Educator — so every category covers every age band, group size, and setting. Every one of the 5,330 activities is a complete standalone card: description, materials, steps, developmental goal, conversation starter, and an educator tip. You'll recognize classics every educator uses, alongside plenty you won't find anywhere else.

Will there be more updates? +

Yes. New modules in the roadmap — HomeEd, SportsEd, SoothED (mental health), CalmED. New activities added to existing modules monthly. All updates are free for current license holders. You'll get an email when each ships.
